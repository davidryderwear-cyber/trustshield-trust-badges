# TrustShield — Demo Video / Screencast Script

**Target length:** 60–90 seconds. **Format:** screen recording with voiceover (or on-screen captions). English audio or English subtitles is required by Shopify.

**Recording tips**
- Record at 1920×1080. Use the **TrustShield Test Store** (already set up, 3 badges, app embed active).
- Hide the browser bookmarks bar; use a clean profile. Zoom the Shopify admin to ~100%.
- Keep the cursor movements slow and deliberate. Pause ~1s on each key screen.
- Tool suggestions: Loom, ScreenStudio, or OBS. Trim dead air at the start/end.

---

### Shot 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)
**On screen:** Storefront product page (The Collection Snowboard) with the trust badges visible below "Buy it now".
**VO:** "Shoppers abandon carts when they don't trust your store. TrustShield fixes that in under two minutes."

### Shot 2 — Install / dashboard (0:08–0:18)
**On screen:** App dashboard with the Setup guide ("1 of 3 steps", "You have 3 badges configured").
**VO:** "Install TrustShield and you're guided through setup. Your first badge set is pre-configured — Free Shipping, Easy Returns, Secure Checkout — so you're basically done already."

### Shot 3 — Enable the app embed (0:18–0:26)
**On screen:** Click "Activate in theme editor" → theme editor shows the TrustShield app embed toggle ON → Save.
**VO:** "Turn on the app embed once, and badges appear on your storefront automatically. No code, no theme edits."

### Shot 4 — Customize in the editor (0:26–0:45)
**On screen:** Open the badge editor. Show the **Content** tab (badge types: Single banner, Icon block, Minimal icons, Payment icons). Switch to the **Design** tab; apply a template (e.g. "Ocean"), tweak a color — the **live preview on the right updates instantly**.
**VO:** "Pick from four badge styles and 36 built-in icons. Match your brand with one-click design templates, custom colors, and spacing — and watch it all update live as you edit."

### Shot 5 — Placement (0:45–0:52)
**On screen:** **Placement** tab — show "All products / specific products / collections / tags" targeting options.
**VO:** "Show badges everywhere, or target specific products, collections, or tags."

### Shot 6 — The result on the storefront (0:52–1:05)
**On screen:** Storefront product page; the badges sit cleanly below the buy buttons. Scroll slightly to show they look native.
**VO:** "The result: clean, professional trust signals exactly where customers decide to buy — building confidence and lifting conversions."

### Shot 7 — Plans + close (1:05–1:15)
**On screen:** Pricing page (Free / Starter / Essential / Professional).
**VO:** "Start free. Upgrade for unlimited badges, A/B testing, and more. Install TrustShield today and turn browsers into buyers."

### Do / Don't (Shopify review)
- ✅ DO show the real app UI and a real storefront result.
- ✅ DO keep claims accurate (don't promise features not in the plan shown).
- ❌ DON'T show competitor apps, fake reviews, or unverifiable stats on screen.
- ❌ DON'T include audio music you don't have rights to.

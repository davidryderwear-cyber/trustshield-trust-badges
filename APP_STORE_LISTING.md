## Detailed Description

### Build Trust. Boost Conversions.

TrustShield adds beautiful, customizable trust badges to your Shopify store in under 2 minutes. Show customers your store is safe, reliable, and trustworthy — right where they make purchase decisions.

### Why Trust Badges Matter
Studies show that 17% of shoppers abandon carts due to security concerns. Trust badges placed near buy buttons can increase conversion rates by up to 42%.

### Key Features

**4 Badge Styles**
- Icon Block — Classic row of icons with labels
- Single Banner — Full-width banner with icon and text
- Minimal Icons — Clean icons without text
- Payment Icons — Show accepted payment methods

**36 Built-in Icons**
Professional SVG icons across 8 categories: Security, Payment, Shipping, Quality, Trust, Reviews, Compliance, and Features. No design skills needed.

**Live Preview Editor**
See your badges update in real-time as you customize. Change colors, sizes, layout, and spacing — see the result instantly.

**Smart Defaults**
New badges come pre-configured with the most popular setup (Free Shipping, Easy Returns, Secure Checkout) so you're live in seconds.

**Design Templates**
One-click design presets: Minimal, Classic, Ocean, Forest, Amber, Purple, Rose, and Dark. Apply a template and fine-tune from there.

**Product & Cart Display**
Show badges on product pages below the buy button AND in the cart drawer to reinforce trust at checkout.
